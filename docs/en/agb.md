### 7.1 Payment methods

| Payment method | Availability |
|---|---|
| Online prepayment (credit card, 3-D Secure) | With online booking via the booking form |
| SEPA direct debit | With online booking (for customers with a German/European account) |
| Cash payment | On site at pick-up |
| Card payment (on site) | On site at pick-up |

For online bookings, the deposit or the full booking amount is due before the booking is finally confirmed. Payment is processed through the payment service provider of our booking system (SimplyBook.it, handled via Stripe/PayPal). The booking only becomes binding once the payment has been received.

Where payment is made in cash on site, the full booking amount is due at pick-up.

### 7.3 Vouchers and refunds

Refunds in the event of cancellation are made in accordance with the conditions in § 5 to the original payment method.
