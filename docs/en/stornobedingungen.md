# Cancellation Policy — CreteCycling

> **Note.** The conditions match the project docs and the General Terms and Conditions (`docs/en/agb.md`, § 5). Cancellation percentages are market-standard. Translation of `docs/stornobedingungen.md`; the German text is authoritative.

---

## Overview

| Service | Up to 24/48 h before | After that | Cancelled by CreteCycling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicycle rental | 100% refund | 50% | 100% |
| Tours | 100% (up to 24 h) | 0% | 100% |
| Workshops | 100% (up to 48 h) | 50% | 100% |

---

## Bicycle rental

- **Up to 24 hours before pick-up:** Full refund.
- **After that:** 50% of the booking amount.
- **If the bicycle is damaged:** The deposit is retained; damage going beyond that is settled within the limits set by law.

## Tours

- **Up to 24 hours before the tour starts:** Full refund.
- **After that:** No refund.
- **Weather cancellation (heavy rain):** Full refund, or rescheduling to an alternative date free of charge.

## Workshops

- **Up to 48 hours before:** Full refund.
- **After that:** 50% of the booking amount.
- **Cancelled by CreteCycling:** Full refund.

---

## How do I cancel?

Send an email to **info@cretecycling.eu** including the following details:

1. Booking number (if available)
2. Name
3. Date of the booking (rental / tour / workshop)
4. Reason (optional)

The cancellation takes effect once we have confirmed it in writing.
